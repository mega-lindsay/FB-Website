What Shawn Will Cover

A Plain-English Tour of Your Federal Benefits

In the "Hour of Power" briefing, Shawn McCoy explains key federal benefit concepts, rules, and considerations. The information is general education and does not promise a particular financial outcome.

FEGLI Life Insurance

  • How Basic, Option A, B & C actually work
  • What happens to your coverage at retirement
  • The most common FEGLI mistakes federal employees make
  • How to calculate if you're over- or under-insured
FEGLI Option B costs generally increase with age, which can surprise employees later in their career.

TSP Retirement

  • How the G, C, S, I and L funds actually differ
  • How to optimize contributions for your timeline
  • The FERS supplement — who gets it and when it stops
  • What to do with your TSP when you retire
Many federal employees may not fully understand how their TSP fund choices affect long-term growth.

FEHB Health Insurance

  • How to actually compare FEHB plans (not just the premium)
  • Open season tips to lower your monthly cost
  • FEHB + Medicare coordination explained clearly
  • TRICARE for retired federal workers with military service
FEHB plan comparisons should consider more than just the monthly premium.

Meet Your Webinar Host

"Dr. FedEd" Shawn McCoy

President & Director of National Development

Shawn McCoy has over 39 years of experience in the financial services industry, starting his career with MetLife in Carrollton, Texas, where he worked with Federal employees. He has held roles in management, group sales, compliance, and wholesaling and has delivered public seminars on Federal employee benefits, financial and investment topics nationwide.

For the past 17 years, Shawn has conducted over 1,500 briefings across the U.S and the world, educating over 40,000 Federal employees on retirement, life, health, and other government benefits. He also collaborates with local financial advisors to enhance their understanding of Federal benefits programs. As a former registered representative, Shawn held Series 7, 63, 24, and 65 licenses.
